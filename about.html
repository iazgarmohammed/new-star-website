About New Star Enterprises

A decade of manufacturing uniforms that professionals trust.

Built on Precision & Trust

New Star Enterprises was founded in Chennai with a single purpose — to manufacture uniforms that professionals could genuinely rely on. Not just fabric stitched together, but garments engineered for the demands of operating theatres, busy kitchens, and school corridors.

Over the past decade, we have grown from a local Chennai supplier to a pan-India manufacturer serving 500+ hospitals, hotel chains, food franchises and educational institutions. Every order — whether one piece or ten thousand — receives the same attention to fabric quality, colour consistency and finishing.

Our WhatsApp-first order process is not a workaround — it is a deliberate choice. We believe that uniform procurement should involve conversation, not just a checkout cart. Bulk pricing, custom embroidery, size charts — these decisions are better made together.

Our Journey
