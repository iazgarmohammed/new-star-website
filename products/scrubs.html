Fabric & Care Details

Everything you need to know about your scrubs.

🧵

Fabric Composition

65% Polyester / 35% Cotton medical-grade blend. Moisture-wicking, breathable and colour-fast through repeated washes.

🫧

Care Instructions

Machine wash at 60°C. Tumble dry low. Iron on medium heat. Do not bleach. Colour-safe wash recommended for dark colours.

✅

Standards & Fit

Available in XS to XXL and custom sizes. Unisex fit. Suitable for OT, ward, surgical and clinical environments.
